Session 25 — Tuck Handstand, Chest Killer & Typewriter Pulls
~1h35. A skill day introducing the tuck handstand and the typewriter pull-up (an advanced pull toward one-arm work), plus the chair hold, the two-stage chest-killer isometric pyramid, and an optional straight-arm plank on the bar.
~1h35 ★ Tuck handstand ★ Typewriter pull-ups Chest killer iso Chair hold
🎙 Coach's message
"New stuff: the tuck handstand — we've done diamond and straddle but never tuck. It's brutal on the traps and lower back. And the typewriter pull-up: wide grip, thumbs over the bar, chin over, then slide side to side — each slide is a rep, never coming back down. Big note: don't force the far arm straight yet. Give your tendons a few sessions to adapt first, or use a band. Archer push-ups in the warm-up are essential to protect you here."

Typewriter Pull-ups NEW
6–8 sets × 4–6 controlled reps
15 min▾
Typewriter — slide side to side, chin stays over the bar
Coach's key advice — read this
Wide grip, thumbs over the bar. Pull to chin-over-bar, then slide to one side and back — each slide is one rep, never dropping down. The bent arm takes 80–90% of the load; the other extends only at the end for balance. Do NOT force that far arm fully straight yet — give your tendons a few sessions to adapt first, or use a band to shed load. 4–6 reps per set, in control (one slide = one rep).

Straight-Arm Plank on Bar (optional)
3 sets × 2 min · vary every 10–20s
10 min▾
Shoulder-joint prep for flags
Optional, only if you have energy. Straight-arm plank on the push-up bar, 2 min per set. Every 10–20s, vary one element: two arms → one arm, remove a foot, switch pronated ↔ supinated grip, or walk the feet forward. Too easy? Add a weighted vest. This conditions the shoulder joints for the flag work coming later.

Session 26 — Degressive Iso & Inverted Pyramids
~1h30. Muscle-ups switch to AMRAP, then two inverted pyramids (squats and push-ups) where difficulty ramps back up at the end, and a new two-stage grip killer run as a degressive protocol — hold to failure, then add assistance and keep going.
~1h30 ★ MU AMRAP Inverted pyramids Degressive grip killer
🎙 Coach's message
"Muscle-ups go to AMRAP on purpose — max reps in the window. Option 1 is 10 min with a 15kg band (aim 20–30); option 2 is 15 min bodyweight (aim 15–20). You can start bodyweight and switch to the band if you fail. Squats and push-ups are inverted pyramids — the hard sets come at the end, which is mentally tougher. Speed and tension, minimal rest, don't break the set. New grip killer: two blocks, each a degressive chain — hold bodyweight to failure, grab a light band and continue, then a strong band."

2-Stage Grip Killer — Degressive NEW
2 blocks · hold to failure, add assistance, continue
10 min▾
How the degressive protocol works
Within each block, the 3 sub-holds chain together with no rest: hold to failure at bodyweight, immediately grab a light band (15kg) and hold again, then a strong band (25kg) and hold once more. The assistance increases as you fatigue so you keep accumulating hold time. Rest by feel between the two blocks; you can repeat the blocks if you want more.
