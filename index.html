How Bring Sally Up works
Read this before starting — it's simple once you get it
▾
The song "Bring Sally Up" alternates between the words "up" and "down" throughout its 3:30 runtime. Your job is to switch between two isometric positions every time the word changes. You're never doing reps — you're just holding one position or the other, the whole time.
The challenge: the holds get long in the middle of the song. Your muscles will be screaming. If you fail, take a 5–10s cluster rest and get back in position.
What "clusters" means
  • If you can't hold anymore, take a brief rest (5–10s)
  • Then get back into position and keep following the song
  • Over time the cluster rests get shorter until you can do it unbroken
Today's goal: survive 1:30–1:45 of the song. That's already a success for your first attempt.
1
Bring Sally Up — Squats
Song: 3:30 · target: survive 1:30+ · 5 min rest after
5 min ▾
The two positions
Position HIGH
Standing upright — legs straight
Position LOW
Squat hold — hips at 90°, parallel to floor
Choose your option
Option 1 — Opposite (easier)
"UP" in song → hold LOW position
"DOWN" in song → hold HIGH (standing)
Option 2 — Follow literally (harder)
"UP" in song → hold HIGH (standing)
"DOWN" in song → hold LOW position
Option 1 is harder on your legs because you spend more time in the low position when the song is most intense. Option 2 feels more intuitive. Start with Option 1.
5 min rest after squats

Bring Sally Up — Push-ups
Song: 3:30 · target: survive 1:30–1:45 · band assist OK
5 min ▾
The two positions
Position HIGH
Arms fully extended — top of push-up position
Position LOW
Arms at 90° — chest near floor, hovering
Choose your option
Option 1 — Opposite
"UP" in song → hold LOW (arms bent)
"DOWN" in song → hold HIGH (arms straight)
Option 2 — Follow literally (harder)
"UP" in song → hold HIGH (arms straight)
"DOWN" in song → hold LOW (arms bent)
Band assistance: Attach a resistance band to the pull-up bar, loop it under your belly/hips. This reduces your effective bodyweight and lets you hold longer. Reduce the band over time.
If you're outdoors: the coach suggests looping the band around a tree branch and passing it around your waist.

3
Bring Sally Up — Plank
Song: 3:30 · no options — fixed positions · commando plank
5 min ▾
No options here — there is only one version. The positions are fixed.
Fixed positions — no choice
"UP" in song
Straight-arm plank — hands on floor, arms extended
"DOWN" in song
Elbow plank — forearms on floor
This is called "commando plank" or "dynamic plank" — alternating between straight-arm and elbow plank following the song's rhythm. Core stays tight throughout.
If you want to repeat any exercise a second time after all three — you can. Coach says this is optional for if you want to push despite the deload.
