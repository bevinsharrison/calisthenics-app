Chin-ups + Isometric — EMOM NEW
10 min · supination grip · palms facing you
10 min ▾
CHIN-UPS = supination grip. Hands on the bar with palms facing toward you. This is different from pull-ups (pronation). Same EMOM structure, same options — but a new movement pattern.
Choose your option
Option 1 — Reps → then hold
5 chin-ups, then 8s isometric hold at the top (chin above bar). Easier to pace.
Option 2 — Hold → then reps ↑ harder
8s iso hold first (chin above bar), then 5 chin-ups. Arms more pre-fatigued for reps.
If it gets hard
  • Use half-reps — partial range still counts, do not break the EMOM
  • Use a resistance band (-15kg light / -25kg strong) to assist
  • Reduce iso hold to 5s if 8s is too much
  • Do NOT break the EMOM — the goal is adapting to this duration + intensity
To go further: push to 6 reps instead of 5. The EMOM duration is already 10 min (up from 8 min vs. last session).

Clap Push-ups NEW
6–8 sets × 12 reps · 1:30–2:00 rest · stay explosive
12 min ▾
Clap push-ups = plyometric push-ups. Push explosively enough to leave the ground, clap your hands, then land and return to position. These develop explosive upper body power — a different quality than regular push-ups.
How to do them
  • Lower controlled, then explode up hard enough to get airborne
  • Clap once, then land with bent arms to absorb impact
  • Reset and repeat — don't rush between reps
  • If you can't get airborne: do fast push-ups with a hard push at the top instead
