V-Ups — EMOM NEW
10 min · 10 reps/min · feet never touch ground
10 min ▾
What a V-Up looks like Lying flat, simultaneously lift your legs and upper body to form a V shape — hands reach toward feet. Lower back down but keep feet just above the floor. The V is the target.
The key rule — continuous tension
  • Feet never rest on the floor during the set — they touch but don't press down
  • This keeps core under tension continuously across all 10 reps
  • Exhale on the way up, inhale on the way down
  • If EMOM breaks: stop the timer, finish the 100 total reps (10 min × 10 reps) at your own pace
This is also upper body recovery time before Pull-ups Block 2. Don't burn your arms here.

Pull-ups — Block 3 of 3 (AMRAP)
15 min total · Block 1: BW 8 min → Block 2: Australian 7 min
15 min ▾
AMRAP = As Many Reps As Possible within the time limit. You set the pace — do sets, rest briefly, go again. This is how you close out the 100 reps.
Two blocks — no rest between them
Block 1 — 8 min
Bodyweight pull-ups
Do as many pull-ups as you can. If you started at ~64 reps, you need ~36 more to hit 100. Sets of 5–8 with short rests is a reasonable strategy.
Block 2 — 7 min
Australian pull-ups
Switch immediately to Australian pull-ups (bar at waist height, body inclined, pull chest to bar). These let you keep going even when bodyweight pull-ups are done.
What are Australian pull-ups?
Set a bar at about waist height. Hang below it with straight arms, body inclined (feet on floor). Pull your chest up to the bar. The more horizontal your body, the harder it is. Also called "inverted rows" or "low bar pull-ups".
Do NOT rest between Block 1 and Block 2. Just switch exercise and keep going.
AMRAP timers — run them back to back

Muscle-ups NEW
Free system · 10–15 reps total · BW or assisted
10 min ▾
Free system means no EMOM, no pyramid — just accumulate 10–15 total reps however you can. Singles are fine. Doubles are fine. Whatever lets you hit the volume with good technique.
Assistance options
  • Bodyweight — use this if you can, even for some reps
  • Light band (-15kg) — first fallback
  • Heavy band (-25kg) — if the light band isn't doable yet
The long-term progression (coach's roadmap)
NOW
Free system — accumulate 10–15 reps any way possible (singles, doubles, with band)
Next
Push volume to 20 → 25 → 30 reps in free system with same band level
Then
Switch to EMOM system (e.g. 3 reps/min for 10 min) — adds intensity at same volume
Then
Reduce assistance (lighter band) and repeat the cycle from free system again
Goal
Bodyweight EMOM 3 reps/min × 10 min = 30 unassisted muscle-ups

Tricep Push-ups NEW
Free system · 50 total reps · from plank on elbows
10 min ▾
Not regular push-ups. Start position: plank on elbows and knees (elbows under shoulders, palms flat on floor). End position: same plank but arms fully extended. You're extending at the elbows using only your triceps — no chest or shoulder push involved. This is the push portion of a muscle-up.
Movement cues
  • Start: forearms on the ground, elbows under shoulders
  • Press: extend elbows to push up to straight arms (plank position)
  • Return: lower back to forearms with control
  • Rest between mini-sets as needed — rep by rep if necessary — just accumulate 50

Chest Pull-ups — EMOM NEW
10 min · explosive · chest reaches bar height
10 min ▾
Chest pull-ups (also called "high pull-ups") = pronation grip pull-ups that are explosive enough to bring your upper chest level with the bar. This is the pulling phase that feeds directly into the muscle-up transition.
Technique — two key differences from regular pull-ups
  • Trajectory: you pull at an angle (backward diagonal), not straight up. Your body leans slightly back as you rise — this is what lets your chest clear the bar
  • Elbow position: keep elbows pointing slightly inward (not flared out). Inward elbows let you get higher. Flared elbows are for scapular retraction but block you from reaching chest height
  • Explosiveness: these must be fast and powerful — you cannot slow-rep your chest to bar height
Choose your option
Option 1 — 3 reps/min bodyweight
All 10 minutes at bodyweight. Lower intensity, focus on technique and trajectory.
Option 2 — 3 BW then 5 assisted
First 5 min: 3 reps/min bodyweight. Last 5 min: 5 reps/min with light band. Volume increase mid-session.
Option 3 — 4 reps/min bodyweight
All 10 minutes at bodyweight but 4 reps/min. Harder than Option 1, stays bodyweight.
Coach reduced intensity on purpose here to keep you explosive. Don't go above your option — the goal is quality, not fatigue.

Finisher — 3-stage max effort
Max pull-ups → 3-stage iso → max push-ups · no rest
5 min ▾
Take maximum rest before starting — 4–5 minutes minimum. Have a specific target number in your head before each exercise. Don't start without a goal.
The chain — do not rest between steps
1
Max pull-ups
On your last rep — do NOT lower yourself down. Stay at the top and transition directly to step 2.
2
Hold — chin above bar (top iso)
Hold as long as possible with chin over the bar. When you can't hold the top, lower to the middle.
3
Hold — head under bar (middle iso)
Head level with the bar, hold as long as possible. When done, lower to dead hang.
4
Deadhang — bottom iso
Arms fully straight, hold as long as possible. Then drop and go directly to push-ups.
5
Max push-ups — floor
Immediately drop and do max push-ups. You're done when you can't push anymore.
