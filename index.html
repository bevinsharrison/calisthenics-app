Deload week — recovery is the goal, not progress. Plank endurance, light muscle-ups, and express pull circuits. ★★★★★

🌙
This is a deload week — rest is the point Quentin was explicit: your main goal this week is to recover, not to progress. Don't rush into every exercise. There's content here so you have something to do, but feel free to do less. Listen to your body and take the rest.
Session 13 — "Tourne, retourne et c'est cuit !"
~1h10 total. Quentin's theme: you're a piece of meat being cooked in the kitchen. A double EMOM of pull-ups + squats, then three blocks of plank cycles that shift from short-and-intense (block 1) to lower-intensity-but-higher-volume (block 3).
Deload · ~1h10 Double EMOM Plank cycles ×3 Intensity → volume switch
🎙 Coach's message
"You're like food being cooked in the kitchen for this one. The first blocks are short but intense — endurance in the 'intensity' sense. As you go deeper, hold time per set drops but total work time rises because there are more cycles. So you switch to lower intensity, endurance in the 'cumulative hold time' sense. That's the main switch in this session."

The cycle rule
No rest inside a cycle — the exercises run back-to-back. Rest as much as needed between cycles, and take a big rest between blocks. The strategy shifts from short/intense (block 1) to lower-intensity/high-volume (block 3).

Movement reference
Superman: face down, arms and legs lifted off the floor. Hollow: on your back, feet and upper back off the floor. Copenhagen: side plank with the top foot on an elevated support. Iso push-up: hold the bottom of a push-up (90° elbow, not touching floor). Dynamic: switch elbows ⇄ straight arms as fast as possible.

Session 14 — Muscle-Ups & Express Pull Circuits
~1h05 total. Light deload muscle-up volume (10–12 reps, or 8–12 fine), then two express pull circuits and an optional double pyramid of pull-ups + push-ups. Lots of isometric prep work in the warm-up.
Deload · ~1h05 Muscle-ups 2 express circuits Double pyramid
🎙 Coach's message
"This is deload, so light on the muscle-ups. The important thing is progressively adding more MUs each session — build volume. Once you reach 15–20 reps, we can introduce EMOM systems and supersets combining muscle-ups and bar dips. But for now, keep it light. Use next week to rest — don't rush into every exercise."
