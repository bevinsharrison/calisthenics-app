Cossack squats (unilateral)
EMOM · 10 min · 8 reps per leg, per minute
10 min ▾
Also called squats unilatéraux. One leg straight, the other does the work — improves adductor activation and balance. Audio cue: 70/30 weight distribution, not 50/50. The straight leg takes about 30%, the bent (working) leg takes 70%.
70/30 cue (from audio): Don't split the load 50/50 between legs. Keep one leg straight (30%) and load the other (70%). That's what builds the strength — putting more weight on a single side.
Good execution > forcing it. Don't push through the knees if form breaks.

Explosive pull-ups · the PULL
EMOM · 10 min · 3–4 reps per minute · stay explosive
10 min ▾
Low reps on purpose. Coach wants you explosive in every single rep — even at minute 8. Don't grind. If you can't be explosive on rep 4, do 3.
Goal: get your chest to the level of the bar (at least theoretically — get as close as you can). This is the upward propulsion of a muscle-up trained on its own.
The trajectory cue (audio + PDF)
Regular pull-up vertical · elbows out
Explosive pull-up diagonal · elbows in & forward
The trick (audio): on a regular pull-up your elbows flare out and you go straight up — but the bar gets in your way. For explosive, keep your elbows in and oriented forward on the way up. Your body moves slightly away from the bar in a diagonal arc. This is what lets you get high enough for a muscle-up.

Pullover + negative muscle-ups · the TRANSITION
Free system · 12–15 reps · slow the descent as much as possible
10 min ▾
This is the trickiest piece of the muscle-up — getting your shoulders over the bar. You'll train it two ways: a pullover to get above the bar, then a slow negative to feel the transition under control on the way down.
What's a pullover? (from audio) A different way to get above the bar — feet go up first, then your head follows. Reverse path of a muscle-up. If unclear, search "pullover calisthenics" on YouTube — coach said to message him for a video if needed.
How to do this block
  • Step 1: Pullover yourself above the bar (any way that works)
  • Step 2: Slow negative muscle-up on the way down — focus the transition, fight the descent
  • Step 3: Drop, rest, repeat
  • 12–15 reps total · rep by rep is fine · no need to chain them
  • Take as much rest as you need between reps
Form alert: Slow the descent as much as possible — but stop if you feel pain in the shoulder during the transition. Don't push through joint discomfort.

From your last pull-up: chin over the bar hold → head under the bar hold → dead hang (straight arms). Hold each as long as you can.
