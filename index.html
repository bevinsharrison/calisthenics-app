Chin-ups & Dead Hang — Ashura ENDURANCE
Pyramid · Bloc 1 finds N · Bloc 2 repeats N times · chin-ups now
10 min▾
How the Ashura works
This time: chin-ups (supinated grip), not pull-ups.

Bloc 1 — one continuous set: 1 chin-up → 1s dead hang → 2 → 2s → 3 → 3s → ... until failure. No rest. The stage you fail at = N.

Bloc 2 — N sets: each repeats the Bloc 1 structure until failure. At least 2:30 rest between sets. You'll decline across sets — that's normal.

Count dead hang from 0! The hang is active recovery — forearms still gripping.

One continuous set. No rest. Chin-ups (supinated). Go until failure.

Lift Day — Hypertrophy for the Gymnast Look
~50 min, straight sets (no supersets, so you keep your stations). Slot this between Quentin's two sessions so all three days are evenly spaced. The goal here is visible muscle — the controlled tempo and 8–15 rep ranges are what your skill work doesn't prioritize.
~50 min Straight sets Hypertrophy 8–15 reps Controlled tempo
📋 How this fits
"This complements Quentin's programming — it doesn't replace it. The exercise selection deliberately avoids heavy overhead pressing and pull-up variations since he hammers those already. The gymnast look is shoulders, upper chest, back width, and arms over a lean base — that's what these movements target. Progress by adding small weight, not reps. And this only works if you're eating enough protein: ~130–145g/day."
One note on timing
You just finished a deload week. Start this third day on a normal training week, not a recovery one. Two hard pulling/pushing sessions plus this should still leave a rest day or two — if you feel beat up, drop a set off each exercise rather than skipping.
