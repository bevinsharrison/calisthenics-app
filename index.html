Pull-ups — Ashura Pyramid Series
5 series · reps + dead hang · never let go
15 min▾
How to read each series
Format is reps'/dead-hang'. So "3-3'/2-2'" = 3 pull-ups → 3s dead hang → 2 pull-ups → 2s dead hang, never releasing the bar. At least 2:30 rest between series. These build toward 15+ reps in a row. If you fail, retry with a band; to go further, add 5kg.

Session 20 bis — Handstand (at the end), L-Sit & Archer Pulls
~1h35. Same skill toolkit as 20, reordered on purpose: L-sit EMOM (with a new tuck-to-full option), archer pull-ups, squats, a push-up EMOM — and then the handstand last, so pre-fatigued shoulders make it deliberately harder.
~1h35 ★ Handstand moved to END ★ Tuck→Full L-sit Archer pulls 12–16
🎙 Coach's message
"The interesting change: we invert the handstand — normally it's first, this time it's last, after the push-ups. Handstand and push-ups both use the front shoulders, so the push-up EMOM will pre-fatigue you and make the handstand harder, on purpose. Training the fine forearm adjustments under fatigue is good for your handstand. New L-sit option 2: tuck-to-full transition, 8 controlled reps/min with a brief hold at each end. Archer pull-ups slightly higher reps. Push-ups: same two options — I don't want to push too hard with the handstand waiting at the end."
