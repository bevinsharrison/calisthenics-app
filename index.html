Session 23 — "Ups and Downs!"
~50 min. A deload built around the Bring Sally Up challenge (the 3:37 YouTube song) run three ways: squats, push-ups, and plank. Each rep syncs to the song — "up" and "down" trigger different isometric holds.
Deload · ~50 min ★ Bring Sally Up ×3 Endurance challenge
🎙 Coach's message
"Bring Sally Up is a famous 3:37 YouTube challenge — a mix of push-ups and isometric holds synced to the song. Do it in squats, push-ups, and plank. Rest 3–5 min after each one, especially the upper-body ones. For squats you can probably go the whole song. For push-ups, aim for 1:40 — 1:45 to 2:00 is already excellent at this stage; reaching the end takes weeks. It's a deload, but if you want more, rest 5 min and retry on parallettes."

Two ways to push further
1 · Band assist: loop a resistance band to reduce load so you can pass 2 min, then reduce the assistance week by week.
2 · Clusters: can't do the full 3:37? Split it — e.g. 2:00 + 1:37 with a short rest between. Shrink the rest week after week until it's continuous.

Commando plank version
"Up" = hold the straight-arm plank (hands on floor). "Down" = hold the elbow plank. Switching between them on cue is essentially a commando / dynamic plank. Rest 3–5 min after if you're chaining challenges.

Session 24 — Human Flag, Muscle-Ups & Core Killer
~1h30. A skill day: muscle-up EMOM with three assistance options, static lunges, the T-Flag and Human Flag progressions, and a new "stage 3 bis" isometric core-killer pyramid.
~1h30 ★ T-Flag ★ Human Flag plank Core killer iso MU EMOM
📋 Session focus
Skill day toward the human flag. Warm up the obliques, lats, and scapula specifically before the T-Flag. Two hard rules: the pushing (bottom) arm must stay locked straight, and the pulling (top) hand grip must stay locked on the bar. Pick MU EMOM options to match your level — you can even mix two options across the 10 minutes.
