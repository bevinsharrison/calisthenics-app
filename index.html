Session 19 — Muscle-Up & Pull-up Endurance Protocol
~1h40. Three descending-difficulty MU blocks to failure, alternating (plyometric) squats EMOM, regular dips EMOM, an Ashura-variant pull-up pyramid, and a knee-raise pyramid for core + grip.
~1h40 ★ 3 MU blocks to failure Alternating squats Ashura pull-up pyramid
📋 Session focus
Endurance protocol. The MU difficulty steps down across the three blocks (bodyweight → light band → strong band) so you adapt the exercise to accumulating fatigue and keep hitting failure with volume. The pull-up series are variants of the Ashura endurance routine — finish each pyramid as fast as possible without releasing the bar.

Pull-ups — Ashura Pyramid Series
5 series · reps + dead hang · finish fast, never let go
15 min▾
How to read each series
Format is reps'/dead-hang'. So "3-3'/2-2'" = 3 pull-ups → 3s dead hang → 2 pull-ups → 2s dead hang. Never release the bar within a series. At least 2:30 rest between series. Series 1–4 are variants of series 5 (the original Ashura routine). Finish each as fast as possible.

Session 20 — Handstand, L-Sit & Archer Pull-ups
~1h30. A skill-focused day: handstand practice with form correction, an L-sit EMOM with three progressions, archer pull-ups (one-arm progression), the Bring Sally Up squat challenge, and an elevated push-up EMOM.
~1h30 ★ Handstand form work ★ Archer pull-ups L-Sit EMOM Bring Sally Up
📋 Session focus
Skill day. Handstand is the centerpiece — pick the option matching your progression and film yourself from the side and front to catch left/right imbalances. Archer pull-ups are a big step toward the one-arm pull-up; be careful, they're very demanding on the tendons, so warm up thoroughly.

Handstand SKILL
Free system · pick your progression option
15 min▾
Form correction — open the shoulder angle to make a line
Form cues
Spread your fingers wide and grip the floor ("spider" hand) for stability and control. Open the shoulder angle, do a posterior pelvic tilt (tuck the hips), look between your hands, and point your toes. The correction shifts your center of gravity forward — anticipate it by pressing into the floor with your fingertips. Film from the side (fix the banana) and the front (check left/right balance). Train both on the floor and on parallettes.

Tendon caution
Very demanding on the tendons — make sure you're warmed up. Unlike regular pull-ups, keep the thumb over the bar when you grab it. The non-pulling arm stays as straight as possible — it's a stabilizer. Use a band if needed. Rest by feel. A step toward the one-arm pull-up.

The challenge
