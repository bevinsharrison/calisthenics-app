Pull-ups + Isometric — EMOM
8 minutes · new: iso hold added each minute
8 min ▾
Each minute, you do pull-up reps AND hold an isometric. The order depends which option you choose. Both options add difficulty compared to Session 1 — the iso hold means less rest.
Choose your option
Option 1 — Reps then hold
5 pull-ups, then 8s isometric hold at the top · easier to pace
Option 2 — Hold then reps
8s isometric hold first, then 5 pull-ups · harder, arms more fatigued for reps
What the isometric hold means
  • Hold your chin above the bar for 8 seconds — this is the top isometric
  • Your grip must stay tight throughout (thumb under bar)
  • If you cannot hit 8s cleanly, reduce to 5s rather than breaking form
To make it harder: push to 10 min EMOM, then go to 6 reps + 5s iso. The progression is built in.

Jump Squats — EMOM
8 minutes · 12 reps each minute
8 min ▾
Same format as Session 1 squats, but now jump squats. Explosiveness is the goal — if you can't stay explosive, slow down rather than doing sloppy bounces.
Prescription
  • 12 jump squats every minute, 8 minutes
  • Land softly, reset, stay controlled between reps
  • If EMOM breaks: finish the reps at your own pace to stay explosive
To go further: push to 10 min EMOM, then add 5s bottom isometric (hips parallel) at the start of each minute — same idea as the pull-up progression.

Knee Raises — EMOM
10 minutes · 8 reps per minute
10 min ▾
Core and grip endurance together. Bonus: knee raises double as scapular pull-ups and deadhang work, so they also train your grip and pulling muscles.
Prescription
  • 8 hanging knee raises every minute for 10 minutes
  • Exhale as knees rise toward your belly
  • Inhale as legs lower back down
  • Slow the lowering phase: 2–3 seconds eccentric
To go further: slow the descent to 2–3s (count from 0). Knee raises train scapular stability and deadhang grip simultaneously.

Pull-ups — Deadstop
5 sets × 10 reps · free rest (min 1:30)
15 min ▾
What is deadstop?
Deadstop = pause for 1 full second at the bottom (arms fully straight) between every rep. This eliminates the elastic rebound from the muscles, making each rep start from zero. Harder than standard pull-ups at the same rep count.
Prescription
  • 5 sets of 10 deadstop pull-ups
  • Free rest — rest by feel, minimum 1:30 between sets
  • Do NOT break sets — use a band (-15kg or -25kg) if needed to keep the set unbroken
  • Focus: building strength endurance in high-rep sets (above 12)
The coach's goal is to get comfortable at 10–15 rep sets. This is a prerequisite for muscle-ups.

Split Squats
5 sets × 20 reps per leg · free rest
15 min ▾
What are split squats?
Split squats = lunge position, but feet stay fixed. You only do knee + hip flexion/extension — no stepping. Stay in the split position for the full set.
Prescription
  • 5 sets × 20 reps per leg
  • Free rest between sets
  • Keep torso upright and stable throughout
  • Knee tracks over the foot, not caving inward
High rep count by design — this builds strength endurance in the legs to mirror the high-rep pull-up work.

Push-up Variations — with tempo
8 sets · NEW: slow eccentric on first 4 sets
10 min ▾
What tempo notation means
Tempo format = X-0-X-0 (eccentric / pause / concentric / pause). 30X0 = 3s lowering / no pause / explode up / no pause. 20X0 = 2s lowering / no pause / explode up. X0X0 = normal speed both ways.
Sets Variation Reps Tempo
1 & 2 Elevated (feet on box) 12 30X0 — slow 3s down
3 & 4 Diamond 12 20X0 — slow 2s down
5 & 6 Wide (60° angle) 15 X0X0 — normal
7 & 8 Regular (45° angle) 15 X0X0 — normal
New this session: eccentric control on elevated and diamond. 3s down for elevated, 2s down for diamond. This is where the difficulty sits — don't rush it.
If wrists hurt on elevated: use parallettes or handles.

Finisher
NEW: max pull-ups → max deadhang → max push-ups
5 min ▾
Upgraded from Session 2. Now three consecutive max efforts: pull-ups, then hang the bar as long as possible, then push-ups — no rest between them.
Sequence
  • Rest 3–4 min first (use timer below)
  • Max pull-ups — go all out
  • No rest — immediately go to max deadhang
  • No rest — immediately drop and max push-ups
Have a realistic target in mind before you start. If your fresh max is 15 pull-ups, expect ~12 post-workout. Set the number before you get on the bar.
