Muscle-Ups
Free system · 15–20 reps accumulated
10 min▾
Progression path
Try bodyweight first. If you can't reach 15–20, use the 15kg band. If still too hard, move to 25kg. NEVER pass one arm then the other over the bar — always lock your grip (thumb UNDER the bar). Focus on the swing timing: generate a pendulum, trigger the MU on the return swing at point B.

Timing cues
Grip: slightly wider than shoulder-width, thumb under bar. Swing: body forms a line, slight hollow in pulling phase. Arms extended at point B. During the pull, orient elbows forward, follow the bar with your eyes, tuck head into shoulders approaching the transition.

Commando pull-ups
Also called X-grip. Pull toward the arm furthest from you on the bar. Fight the natural body rotation. Uses more chest than regular pull-ups — load is ~70/30 instead of 50/50. Builds toward one-arm pull-ups. Use a 15kg band if needed. For chin-ups, use shoulder-width supinated grip.

Ashura — Pull-ups & Dead Hang ENDURANCE
Pyramid · Bloc 1 finds N · Bloc 2 repeats N times
10 min▾
How the Ashura works
Bloc 1 — ONE continuous set: 1 pull-up → 1s dead hang → 2 pull-ups → 2s dead hang → 3 pull-ups → 3s dead hang → ... continue until failure. No rest between stages. The stage where you fail = N.

Bloc 2 — N sets: Each set repeats the Bloc 1 structure (1/1s → 2/2s → 3/3s → ... until failure). Take at least 2:30 rest between sets. You might reach stage 5 on set 1, then 4, then 4, then 3, then 3 — that's normal.

Count dead hang from 0! The dead hang between stages is active recovery — your forearms are still gripping the bar.
Bloc 1 — Find your N
One continuous set. No rest. Go until failure.
11 pull-up → 1s dead hang
22 pull-ups → 2s dead hang
33 pull-ups → 3s dead hang
44 pull-ups → 4s dead hang
…Continue until failure → that stage = N
Log your Ashura
Why this routine is legendary
It auto-scales to your level forever. A beginner might get N=3, an advanced athlete N=8. The volume of Bloc 2 adjusts accordingly. Keep this routine — you'll revisit it for years.

How it progresses
Minutes 1–5: 1 pseudo muscle-up + 5 bar dips per minute. From minute 6 onward, add 1 bar dip per minute (so min 6 = 6 BD, min 7 = 7 BD...). Continue until you fail on the bar dips. Don't release the bar between PMU and BD. Adjust base BD count (3, 5, 7) if needed.
