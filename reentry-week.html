4-day return-to-training plan

Back in the gym

Nov 17–20 · 45 minutes max per day · Move confidently, finish with energy.

One important rule

If vomiting or diarrhea is still ongoing, or you have fever, dizziness or dehydration, skip the gym. After a stomach bug, return only when you feel recovered; avoid a shared gym for at least 48 hours after vomiting/diarrhea stops. This week keep 2–3 clean reps in reserve and don't chase failure.
