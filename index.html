Push-ups — "The Belt" NEW
Pyramid on 2 bars · 2-2 → 10-10 → 2-2
5 min▾
The Belt
Two bars of different height (or floor + parallettes). Each step: do the reps on the low bar, then the same on the high bar — e.g. "4-4" = 4 low + 4 high. No rest except switching bars (5–10s if needed near the top).
Low bar High bar
Origin
A partner routine from Zef Zakaveli's Barbarians at Wingate Park, Brooklyn. With a partner you alternate bars and the faster one holds a straight-arm plank while waiting. Solo is fine too — tap each step as you clear it.
