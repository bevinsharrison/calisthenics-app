Session 7 bis — Supersets, iso squats, denser grip killer
~1h25 total. The redesign: muscle-ups become a superset with bar dips (don't release the bar between them), squats now have a 10s iso prefix per minute, the grip killer pyramid runs five sets instead of one, and regular dips drop to 6 reps/min but add iso at the end of each set.
~1h25 total ★ Supersets ★ Iso prefix on squats ★ 5-set grip pyramid ★ Iso suffix on dips

"I improved some parts of Session 7. More iso hold on squats. The grip killer is different — more volume due to the inverted pyramid instead of the single pyramid. For muscle-ups you now have supersets — just add bar dips when you're over the bar. For regular dips I decreased reps per minute but added iso at the end of each set."

Session 8 bis — Specific reinforcements for the muscle-up
~1h25 total. The session breaks the muscle-up into three components — pulling (explosive pull-ups), transition (pseudo muscle-ups), pushing (bar dips) — then puts it back together in the finisher. Pseudo muscle-ups, static lunges, and a restructured dips block are all new.
~1h25 total 3-part muscle-up ★ Pseudo MUs ★ Static lunges ★ Mandatory dips block
🎙 Coach's message
"I did not change the explosive pull-ups, that's important. Pseudo muscle-ups are different now. Lunges are now static — 4 sets of 35s hold per side. For bar dips I changed the structure — Bloc 1 is now mandatory with more reps per set. Finisher is something nicer. Or not. You'll see."
What changed vs Session 8
SAMEExplosive pull-ups — kept identical (coach said this is important to preserve)
NEWPseudo muscle-ups — ASAP system, 30–40 reps. Use a support to jump from
MODLunges → static lunges — 4 sets × 35s per side, holding the lowest position
MODBar dips — restructured. Bloc 1 now mandatory (was 10×5 / 5×10 before). Tempos added: 12X0 / 30X0
MODFinisher — now max pull-ups + max push-ups (simpler, sharper)

Explosive pull-ups PULLING
EMOM 10 min · 3–4 reps per minute
10 min ▾
Why this exists
The pulling component of the muscle-up. Small sets to keep maximum explosivity per rep. The aim is to bring your chest up to the bar — at least in theory. Get as close as possible.
Pull-up trajectory — vertical vs diagonal
Cue: orient elbows forward on the way up. Diagonal trajectory away from the bar — that's how you climb without brute force.
