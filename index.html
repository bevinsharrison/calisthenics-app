Session 10 — Superset Pyramids with Decreasing Peaks
Unlimited time. One massive routine: 15 sets across 5 blocks of superset pyramids combining pseudo muscle-ups, bar dips, and pull-ups. Each block's peak is lower than the last — effort climbs within a block but resets (smaller) at each new block. This is a session to keep in your pocket and recycle with real muscle-ups as you progress.
Unlimited time ★ 15-set pyramid 5 blocks PMU + BD + PU supersets
🎙 Coach's message
"I'm perfectly aware that the level needed to finish this pyramid doing pure muscle-ups is really high — that's why it uses pseudo muscle-ups. But keep this session in your pocket. You can recycle it later with as many real muscle-ups as possible. Try real MUs on sets 1, 2, 6, 7, 10, 13, and 15 — the shorter ones. Take as much rest as you need, especially before sets 4 and 5. Good luck."
