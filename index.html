Ice Cream Makers — EMOM
10 min · 2–3 reps/min · pull-up → front lever
10 min▾
Ice cream maker — chin-over-bar rolling out to front lever
The movement
Start chin over the bar (top of a pull-up), then roll the body out and down into a front lever — arms go from bent to fully straight as you extend. Keep the core engaged the whole time. Breathe. A little swing is OK — sync with it to help. 2–3 reps/min, bodyweight if you can; band (15kg, up to 25kg late) if needed, but don't break the EMOM. Search "ice cream makers calisthenics" if you want a video refresher.

Back Lever OPTIONAL
3–5 hold attempts · only if you have energy
8 min▾
Optional & a bit dangerous — prepare properly
Quentin's honest take: it's more an achievement than a useful workout — once you've done it you likely won't repeat it, and the muscles it hits are trainable elsewhere. Do it only if you have energy and warm up specifically (archer push-ups, elbow plank, band work for arms/chest).
How to get into it
Start in a dead hang → go into "skin the cat" (look it up on YouTube) → from there hold a tuck back lever or straddle back lever. 3–5 hold attempts. Scale down to tuck if straddle is too hard.

Front Lever Negatives ECCENTRIC
controlled lower · fight to stay above 45°
10 min▾
Eccentric work after the raises
After the concentric FL raises, switch to negatives — simpler, so they suit the accumulating fatigue. Start from an inverted hang (arms straight, body tight), then resist the fall, fighting so your body doesn't drop below 45° from vertical. Control the lowering over 3–6s.

Pull-ups — Decomposition (short range)
2 blocks × 4 sets · M→H and B→M
15 min▾
Short-range pulls by stage
Deliberately partial-range pulls to strengthen specific points of the pull-up. Bloc 1 (M→H): from Middle to High — work the top/validation of the pull, starting with your head glued under the bar, elbows pointing outward, finishing chin above the bar. Bloc 2 (B→M): from Bottom to Middle — start in a dead hang and work the initiation, finishing at the M or H stage. 10 reps per set.
